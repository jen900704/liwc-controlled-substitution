# Data access

No dataset is redistributed here. Users must request or download each dataset
from its original provider, accept the provider’s current terms, and comply with
all ethics, privacy, and data-use restrictions.

- **DAIC-WOZ** — obtain from the original Distress Analysis Interview Corpus /
  DAIC-WOZ provider under its data-use process.
- **E-DAIC** — obtain from the original Extended DAIC provider under its
  data-use process.
- **EATD-Corpus** — obtain from the original EATD-Corpus provider and follow its
  stated research-use conditions.
- **MODMA** — obtain from the original MODMA provider and follow its access and
  citation requirements.
- **PDCH** — obtain from the original PDCH dataset provider under the applicable
  agreement.

This release does not provide provider URLs because the release was prepared
offline and access locations and terms may change. Verify access instructions
with the original dataset publications/providers.

LIWC dictionaries and word lists are proprietary and are not included.
Researchers must obtain an appropriate LIWC license and create category-level
features within the permitted environment. Do not publish raw participant data,
identifying keys, transcripts, clinical fields, or fold assignments.
